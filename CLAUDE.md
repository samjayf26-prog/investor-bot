# Rules for anyone (human or Claude) changing this bot

- Keys come only from GitHub Actions secrets (ALPACA_PAPER_*, ALPACA_LIVE_*, ANTHROPIC_API_KEY). Never commit keys, never put them in chat or give them to a Claude run.
- Claude's research runs write only picks/latest.json, research/journal.md and research/lessons.md. They never edit bot code, config.json or tuning.json.
- tuning.json is written only by the learn workflow (bot/learn.py). bot/tuning.py lists the only settings it may change and their bounds; safety limits (loss caps, drawdown halt, kill switch, trade size, position limits, mode) are never learnable. Every change is logged in research/tuning_log.csv. To undo, revert tuning.json; to freeze learning, set `"learning": false` in config.json.
- Live trading needs both `"mode": "live"` and `"confirm_live": true` in config.json.
- The bot treats picks/latest.json as untrusted: it validates and clamps every field, and a bad file means no new entries, never a crash.
- Every risk limit lives in bot/risk.py as a tested pure function. Run `python -m pytest` before every push.
- Picks: at most 5 open (`max_open_positions`), sized 10/15/20% of equity by conviction (risk.CONVICTION_FRAC), and together never more than 75% of the account (risk.PICKS_MAX_FRAC, risk.picks_room) so the core keeps its share. Sam chose these on 2026-11-09 ("5 smaller slots"); they are not learnable and change only on Sam's word.
- No short selling (account under $2,000) and no bearish trades: puts are dormant (`"options_enabled": false`) and inverse/bear ETFs are refused by bot/picks.py. Leverage exists only in the core (bot/core.py, TQQQ under the volatility-targeted, trend-filtered rule in review/profit-overhaul.md) and only while config "core" has `"leverage": true`, a live-money setting that needs Sam's OK. Picks never buy leveraged ETFs.
- The core's signals and the dip overlay act on every close exactly once: in the 15:35-15:58 ET window from QQQ's last trade, or at the next run if that window was skipped. Never make the core wait a day.
- The Alpaca account is dedicated to this bot: `flatten` closes every position in it.
- Claude inside the bot (bot/advisor.py, config "claude") is a pre-buy news check only: it can veto a pick's entry when the news behind it was reversed, the wrong-if already happened or the stock is halted/under regulatory action, never for price action. It never adds, resizes or exits trades, never touches the core, and fails open (the buy goes ahead) on any API error, unclear answer or when the month's budget (`monthly_budget_usd`, default $15) is spent. Every call is logged in ledger/claude_calls.csv with tokens and cost. Turn it off with `"enabled": false`. `trade.yml -f command=ping-claude` makes one tiny call to prove the key works.
- The hourly news-gap scan (bot/scan.py, scan.yml, 10:50-14:50 ET, config "claude"."scan") is the one Claude-in-the-bot path that may add picks or closes to picks/latest.json: it only adds buy picks (conviction capped at 2, at most 2 per scan) whose 6%+ gap the bot confirms on live Alpaca prices, and closes only for ids that are really open; everything it writes goes through the same bot/picks.py validation and bot/risk.py limits as the research run's picks. It shares the "claude" monthly budget ($75 incl. the pre-buy check) and a failed call changes nothing.
